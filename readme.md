# Sprig — Mindful Coding Pet 🌱

A mindful coding companion that lives in your Explorer sidebar.

## A mindful session

1. **Set an intention**: click "＋ Set an intention" in the panel (or `Sprig: Start Mindful Session`). One small, clear thing. Sprig starts you with one settling breath.
2. **Focus**: Sprig counts only active time and shows your intention while you work.
3. **Take real breaks**: after 25 min Sprig suggests a break with a small idea (stretch, walk, listen, water). When you come back, it checks in: still on the same intention?
4. **Breathe while Claude works**: when files change in a burst you didn't type, Sprig suggests a slow breath instead of watching the screen.
5. **Reflect**: `End session & reflect` asks whether you did it, how you feel, and one note for future you. Everything goes to your **Mindful Journal** (`Sprig: Open Mindful Journal`).

## Everything else

- **22 pets** (Clawd by default): pick one with `Sprig: Choose Pet` (↑/↓ previews each one live) or double-click the pet.
- **Reacts to you**: bounces while you type, hops when you save, cheers when your last error is fixed, dozes off after 3 idle minutes. Click it for a little hop.
- **Counts AI edits too**: file changes on disk (e.g. from Claude Code) count as activity when a folder is open.
- **Focus timer**: counts only *active* coding time and suggests a break after 25 min (configurable).
- **Breaks**: a live countdown, and a notification when the break is over.
- **Breathe With Me**: 4 rounds of guided box breathing (4 in, 4 hold, 4 out, 4 hold).
- **Gentle nudges**: 20-20-20 eye rest, water reminders, and a late-night "go to sleep" note.
- **Pause reminders** for 30 min, 1 h, 2 h or until tomorrow.
- **This week's focus**: a 7-day history of your focus time.
- **Fits any panel**: the pet scales with the panel; short panels switch to a compact side-by-side layout.
- **Status bar**: `🌱 18m` shows your current focus time; click it for the Sprig menu.

Commands (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+P): `Sprig: Open Menu`, `Sprig: Start Mindful Session`, `Sprig: Change Intention`, `Sprig: End Session & Reflect`, `Sprig: Open Mindful Journal`, `Sprig: Show Pet`, `Sprig: Choose Pet`, `Sprig: Breathe With Me`, `Sprig: Start a Break Now`, `Sprig: Pause Reminders…`, `Sprig: Show This Week's Focus`, `Sprig: Reset Focus Timer`.
Settings: search "Sprig" in Settings to change focus/break/reminder intervals.
